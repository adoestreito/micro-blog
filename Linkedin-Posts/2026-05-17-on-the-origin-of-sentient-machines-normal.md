# On the origin of sentient machines — LinkedIn (normal)

**Source:** https://blog.adoestreito.com/  
**Date:** 2026-05-17

---

A model trained on the world's knowledge is impressive—and strangely inert.

You get something like a brain in a jar: wise on paper, but disconnected from the world. Add input and output, and you have a chatbot. Useful. Powerful. Still mostly a conversation partner.

The shift happens when you give it tools: a browser, notes, a database. Suddenly it can act, not only answer. That's the move from chatbot to agent.

The catch is integration. Every tool is its own adapter, its own glue code, its own maintenance. Scale that across a stack and the friction adds up fast.

That's why Model Context Protocol (MCP) matters. A standard way to connect models to context and tools—so agents can reach the systems they need without reinventing the connector each time.

I've been thinking about this arc on my blog: absorb → talk → act → connect.
