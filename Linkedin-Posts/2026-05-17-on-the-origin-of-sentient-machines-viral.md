Your LLM is not "dumb."

It's trapped.

Step 1: Train on everything → you get a brain in a jar. Smart. Static. Useless in the real world.

Step 2: Add chat → powerful parlor trick. Great answers. Zero action.

Step 3: Plug in tools (browser, DB, notes) → now you have an agent. It can do things.

Step 4: Realize the nightmare: every tool needs custom integration. Again. And again.

The fix isn't a bigger model.

It's a universal plug: Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Model + Context + Protocol.

Less glue code. More agents that actually work.

I wrote a short post on this evolution (jar → chatbot → agent → connector):

https://blog.adoestreito.com/

If you're building with agents in 2026, where are you on this ladder?
